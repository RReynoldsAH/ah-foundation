About the Foundation

Sowing Into the Communities Where We Do Business.

The AH Foundation exists to strengthen communities by supporting faith-based and nonprofit organizations that are creating meaningful impact through service, compassion, leadership, and stewardship.

Our Story

A Commitment to Service, Beyond the Workplace

For nearly 30 years, INTEGRITY has built its business around service by providing professional facility solutions that support clean, safe, and sustainable environments.

The AH Foundation extends that commitment to service beyond the workplace. Established by INTEGRITY owner Antoninus Hines, the Foundation was created to intentionally invest in the communities where relationships are built and business is conducted.

Rather than simply giving, the Foundation seeks to build meaningful relationships with organizations that understand their communities and are already doing important work.

Why Partnership Matters

Lasting Impact Comes Through Relationships

Many organizations provide funding. We believe lasting impact comes through relationships.

The AH Foundation seeks to know leaders personally, understand the communities they serve, and remain engaged long after funding is provided. Our goal is not simply to fund programs but to build partnerships that strengthen communities for years to come.
