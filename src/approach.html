Our Partnership Approach

Working Together to Strengthen Communities

The AH Foundation exists to strengthen communities by supporting faith-based and nonprofit organizations that are already making a meaningful difference. We believe the strongest partnerships begin with shared values, meaningful relationships, and a mutual commitment to serving others. For that reason, our approach is intentionally relationship-driven rather than application-driven.

Who We Typically Partner With

The AH Foundation Seeks Organizations That:

These characteristics help guide our partnership decisions.

Demonstrate measurable community impact

Serve with compassion, integrity, and purpose

Align with Christian values and faith-centered service

Maintain strong leadership and financial stewardship

Address meaningful needs within their communities

Value long-term partnership and collaboration

Our Partnership Process

Five Steps, One Relationship-Driven Journey

Identify

We actively seek organizations whose missions and community impact align with Foundation priorities. Organizations may be identified through referrals, community relationships, research, leadership recommendations, or direct engagement.

Connect

The Foundation takes time to learn about organizational leadership, mission, programs, and community impact through meaningful conversations and relationship-building.

Evaluate

Organizations may undergo a review process that considers:

  • Mission alignment
  • Leadership
  • Community impact
  • Stewardship practices
  • Organizational health
  • Strategic fit

Invitation

Organizations being considered for partnership may be invited to complete the AH Foundation application and due diligence process.

Partner & Engage

Approved organizations enter into a partnership relationship that may include:

  • Financial support
  • Ongoing communication
  • Reporting
  • Site visits
  • Partnership development
  • Impact review

The Foundation seeks to remain actively engaged in understanding partnership outcomes.

Partnership Is More Than Funding

The AH Foundation Does Not View Partnership as a Transaction

Funding is one component of partnership, but ongoing engagement, mutual trust, shared values, and measurable impact are equally important. We seek to build meaningful relationships with organizations that understand their communities and create lasting change.

Important Notice: The AH Foundation develops partnerships through a relationship-based process. Initial conversations, inquiries, or submission of organizational information do not guarantee funding, selection, or partnership. Partnership decisions are made at the sole discretion of the Foundation and are based on mission alignment, demonstrated impact, organizational readiness, stewardship practices, and available resources.
